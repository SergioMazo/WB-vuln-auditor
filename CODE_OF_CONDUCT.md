# Code of Conduct

WB Vulnerability Auditor welcomes respectful, authorization-first security
collaboration.

Participants are expected to:

- be considerate, specific, and technically honest;
- critique ideas and code rather than people;
- protect target, client, and reporter privacy;
- avoid sharing credentials or identifying assessment data;
- respect legal scope and coordinated disclosure;
- accept good-faith correction.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or encouragement of unauthorized
access are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and
restrict participation when necessary to protect people or systems.

Sensitive reports must use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
